# Insurance Risk Analytics – Task 1 Report

## 1. Project Overview

This analysis explores 18 months of historical insurance data to understand risk patterns, profitability, and segmentation opportunities for pricing optimization.

---

## 2. Data Summary

- Dataset contains policy, vehicle, client, and claims information
- Key variables include TotalPremium, TotalClaims, Province, Gender, VehicleType

---

## 3. Key Insights

- Provinces show varying risk levels, with some regions having significantly higher loss ratios.
- Vehicle type strongly influences claim severity.
- Gender differences in risk are minimal, suggesting pricing fairness across gender groups.
- Claims and premiums show clear outliers that may affect overall risk distribution.

---

## 4. Risk Analysis Findings

- High-risk vehicle categories contribute disproportionately to total claims.
- Certain provinces exhibit consistently higher loss ratios.
- ZipCode-level variation suggests localized risk differences.

---

## 5. Business Recommendations

- Adjust premiums based on vehicle risk categories.
- Consider geographic risk-based pricing (province-level segmentation).
- Maintain gender-neutral pricing as differences are minimal.
- Investigate outlier claims for fraud or special cases.

---

## 6. Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that insurance risk is primarily driven by geography and vehicle type rather than demographic factors.
